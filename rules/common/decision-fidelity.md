# Decision Fidelity

Honor explicit user choices and persisted decisions without silently replacing them.

## Locked Decisions
- Anything recorded in `contexts/decisions.md` under `## Decisions` is non-negotiable unless the user changes it.

## Deferred Ideas
- Anything recorded under `## Deferred Ideas` must stay out of plans and implementations unless the user re-opens it.

## Discretion
- Anything recorded under `## Claude's Discretion` may be decided locally, but the decision should be documented when it materially changes execution.

## Self-Check
- Confirm each meaningful implementation choice either follows a locked decision or is documented as discretion.
