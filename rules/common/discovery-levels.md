# Discovery Levels

Choose the lightest research depth that still removes meaningful uncertainty.

## Level 0: Existing Pattern Extension
- Use when the task follows an established pattern already present in the codebase.
- Confirm the pattern with targeted search before implementation.

## Level 1: Quick Verification
- Use when the approach is known but syntax, versions, or a single integration detail must be confirmed.
- Keep the research small and attach the source to the plan or state.

## Level 2: Standard Research
- Use when selecting between options, adding a new integration, or touching multiple subsystems.
- Produce reusable findings and record them in `contexts/research-index.md`.

## Level 3: Architectural Research
- Use when decisions affect long-term workflow architecture, shared abstractions, or cross-provider behavior.
- Capture assumptions, rejected options, and decision rationale explicitly.
