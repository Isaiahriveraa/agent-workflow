# Learning Capture

Capture only durable, reusable learnings after verification, explicit review, or a completed learning-loop diagnosis.

## When To Capture
- After successful validation
- After a clear architectural decision
- After a repeatable workflow improvement
- After explicit user correction with a verified reusable lesson
- After critic or eval failure with an evidence-backed diagnosis

## What To Capture
- The decision
- Why it was chosen
- What alternatives were rejected
- Where the source artifact lives
- The failure class when the capture came from a miss
- The reusable prevention rule

## What Not To Capture
- Temporary debugging notes
- Repetitive command transcripts
- Unverified hunches

## Relationship To The Learning Loop
- Use `rules/common/learning-loop.md` to diagnose and write back lessons after failures.
- Use this rule to keep only durable captures and avoid noisy memory drift.
