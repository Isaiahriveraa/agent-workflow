# Learning Capture

Capture only durable, reusable learnings after verification or explicit review.

## When To Capture
- After successful validation
- After a clear architectural decision
- After a repeatable workflow improvement

## What To Capture
- The decision
- Why it was chosen
- What alternatives were rejected
- Where the source artifact lives

## What Not To Capture
- Temporary debugging notes
- Repetitive command transcripts
- Unverified hunches
